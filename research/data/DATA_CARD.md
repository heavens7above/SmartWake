# SmartWake Dataset Documentation & Provenance (DATA_CARD.md)

This data card provides formal provenance, collection methodologies, ethical safeguards, schemas, class balances, and validity limitations across all data tiers evaluated in the SmartWake research project.

---

| Tier | Dataset Name | Ground Truth Type | Sensor Placement | Subjects / Nights | Primary Utility | Status in Paper |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 2. Tier A: Naturalistic Phone Telemetry Benchmark

* **Source & Provenance:** Prospective collection via SmartWake Android client (`termux/logger.py` and `SmartWakeApp`).
* **Collection Method:** Android native background daemon sampling triaxial accelerometer snapshot (`termux-sensor -s Accelerometer -n 1`), battery broadcast receiver, and notification list once every 300 seconds.
* **Placement:** Placed flat on mattress beside sleeping subject, connected to AC charger.
* **Ethics & Consent:** All recordings require written informed consent (`research/DATA_COLLECTION_PROTOCOL.md`), strip all device identifiers to random UUIDs, and record zero audio, visual, or GPS data.
* **Morning Assessment:** Karolinska Sleepiness Scale (KSS, 1–9) and Sleep Inertia Grogginess Scale (SIGS, 1–5).
* **Limitations & Biases:** Mattress dampening variations, dual-occupant bed vibration interference, non-standardized phone hardware accelerometers.

## 3. Tier B: Physiologically Grounded Synthetic Benchmark

* **Source & Provenance:** Generated by `research/synthetic_generator.py` using fixed random seed ($42$).
* **Underlying Physiological Models:**
  - 90–110 minute ultradian NREM-REM cycles (Carskadon & Dement, 2011).
  - Progressive nocturnal decrease of N3 and increase of REM (Feinberg & Floyd, 1979).
  - Stage-dependent motor activity probabilities: Wake ($0.70$), N1 ($0.25$), N2 ($0.08$), N3 ($0.02$), REM ($0.05$) (Basner et al., 2008; Cole et al., 1992).
  - Circadian two-process regulation (Borbély, 1982).
* **Dataset Size:** 6,300 raw 5-minute epochs (70 nights across 5 subjects); 6,295 windowed feature vectors.
* **Class Balance:**
  - Wake ($y=0$): 938 epochs ($14.9\%$)
  - Asleep ($y=1$): 5,357 epochs ($85.1\%$)
* **Ethical Notes:** Wholly synthetic data; generates zero human subject risk.
* **Academic Integrity Caveat:** Strictly restricted to pipeline validation, noise perturbation analysis ($\sigma \in [0.0, 0.5]$), and demonstrating how synthetic benchmarks inflate reported classification accuracy.

## 4. Tier C: PhysioNet Clinical PSG Benchmark

* **Source & Provenance:** PhysioNet open repository (`sleep-accel` v1.0.0, Walch et al., 2019).
* **Official Citation:** Walch, O., Huang, Y., Forger, D., & Goldstein, C. (2019). *Sleep stage prediction with raw acceleration and photoplethysmography heart rate data derived from a consumer wearable device*. Sleep, 42(12), zsz180.
* **Permanent DOI:** `10.13026/kmwq-bh44`
* **License:** Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0 (ODC-BY 1.0).
* **Clinical Protocol & Ground Truth:** Simultaneous full in-laboratory clinical polysomnography (PSG) scored by certified registered sleep technologists according to American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) standards in 30-second epochs:
  - `0`: Wake
  - `1`: Stage 1 Light NREM (N1)
  - `2`: Stage 2 Light NREM (N2)
  - `3`: Stage 3 Slow-Wave Deep Sleep (N3)
  - `5`: Rapid Eye Movement Sleep (REM)
* **Sensor Stream:** Apple Watch triaxial acceleration sampled at approximately 50 Hz. Converted to Euclidean magnitude in $\text{m/s}^2$ ($1g = 9.80665\,\text{m/s}^2$) and subsampled into 5-minute rolling windows aligned with SmartWake's feature extractor.
* **Class Balance (5-minute epochs):**
  - Wake ($y=0$): 108 epochs ($21.3\%$)
  - Sleep ($y=1$): 398 epochs ($78.7\%$)
    - Of which N1: $5.2\%$, N2: $51.8\%$, N3: $23.1\%$, REM: $19.9\%$
* **Validity Threats & Domain Mismatches:**
  1. *Placement Mismatch:* PhysioNet acceleration is recorded from the dominant wrist, whereas SmartWake is deployed on a mattress-laid smartphone. Wrist kinematics exhibit higher high-frequency micro-movement than bed-coupled phone sensors.
  2. *Subject Demographics:* Healthy adults and clinical sleep laboratory referrals; may exhibit higher arousal frequencies than naturalistic home sleepers.

## 5. Standard Tabular Feature Schema (9 Dimensions)

All data pipelines output CSV tables conforming strictly to the 9-dimensional input matrix required by `server/src/modules/sleep.py:build_feature_vector()`:

| Index | Feature Name | Data Type | Units / Range | Physiological / System Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `accel_magnitude_mean` | Float | $\text{m/s}^2$ | Mean kinetic intensity over rolling 30-minute window |
| 2 | `accel_magnitude_std` | Float | $\text{m/s}^2$ | Movement variability across window |
| 3 | `accel_magnitude_max` | Float | $\text{m/s}^2$ | Peak gross motor activity / arousal spike |
| 4 | `zero_crossing_rate` | Float | $[0.0, 1.0]$ | Frequency of acceleration oscillation around mean |
| 5 | `notification_delta` | Float | Counts ($\Delta$) | Digital smartphone engagement proxy |
| 6 | `consecutive_still_count`| Float | Integer count | Streak of consecutive inactive pings (motion $< 0.05$) |
| 7 | `charging` | Float | Binary ($0/1$) | Device tethered to external power supply |
| 8 | `hour_sin` | Float | $[-1.0, 1.0]$ | Circadian solar time proxy ($\sin(2\pi t / 1440)$) |
| 9 | `hour_cos` | Float | $[-1.0, 1.0]$ | Circadian solar time proxy ($\cos(2\pi t / 1440)$) |
| Target | `label` | Integer | Binary ($0/1$) | Sleep status ($0 = \text{Wake}$, $1 = \text{Asleep}$) |
| Auxiliary | `stage` | Integer | Categorical | Clinical PSG stage ($0=\text{Wake}, 1=\text{N1}, 2=\text{N2}, 3=\text{N3}, 5=\text{REM}$) |
